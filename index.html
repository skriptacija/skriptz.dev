FAQ

Some of my frequently asked questions

A quick collection of helpful answers so you can get clarity fast. If there’s anything else you’re wondering about, just reach out!

What type of projects do you work on?

I work on custom website projects, from simple landing pages to full multi-page websites. My focus is on complete web design solutions, including design, development, visual identity, and launch.

How long does a project usually take?

Project timelines depend on the scope and complexity. I always aim to work efficiently, but I take as much time as needed to ensure the final result meets both my standards and the client’s expectations. Quality and satisfaction come first.

What do you need from me to get started?

To get started, I usually need a clear idea of your goals, basic information about your business, and any inspiration or references you may have. I’ll guide you through the rest and help define structure, content, and direction.

How do payments work?

Payments are typically split into two parts. An upfront deposit is required before the project begins, with the remaining balance due upon completion, before the website is launched. Payment details are always agreed on upfront.
